Delete Your Account

In the app

Open Chess Around Town, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm. This is immediate and permanently removes your profile, proposals, messages, ratings, follows, check-ins, and achievements. This action cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or can't sign in, email chessaroundtown@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

Your profile, proposals, messages, ratings, follows, check-ins, and achievements. See our Privacy Policy for more on what we collect and how it's used.
